Examples
========

End-to-end science use cases. Each example is a complete, executed notebook:
download it (button at the top of the page) and adapt it to your needs.

   :gutter: 3

      :link: snia_hubble_diagram

      Simulate, observe and fit SNe Ia with SALT, then build a Hubble
      diagram and its residuals.

   .. grid-item-card:: :octicon:`eye;1.5em;sd-mr-1` Detection efficiency
      :link: detection_efficiency
      :link-type: doc

      redshift, and the selection (Malmquist) bias it induces.

   .. grid-item-card:: :octicon:`telescope;1.5em;sd-mr-1` Observing strategies
      :link: survey_strategy
      :link-type: doc

      Compare wide-and-shallow with narrow-and-deep surveys for transient
      science.

   .. grid-item-card:: :octicon:`stack;1.5em;sd-mr-1` Mixed transient samples
      :link: /combining_transients
      :link-type: doc

      Simulate SNe Ia and core-collapse SNe in a single dataset.
